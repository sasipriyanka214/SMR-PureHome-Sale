SMR PRIMARY ORDER - OVERALL SCHEME UPDATE

1. ENVY scheme slabs are now applied from the actual Admin-maintained Scheme Adjustment table.
2. Multiple SKUs/variants/flavors in the SAME scheme category are combined before selecting the slab.
3. Example: ENVY Deo (All SKUs) with 1 CS + 2 CS = 3 CS uses the 2 CS slab (34%), not the 5 CS slab.
4. Different ENVY scheme categories are calculated separately because the supplied scheme sheet is category-specific.
5. Pure Home remains separate from ENVY and uses its own Admin Scheme Adjustment category rules.
6. Scheme is applied to the product RATE, while MRP remains visible/locked from the product master.
7. Primary Order review, confirmation, legacy Primary Order creation, and Admin Primary Order editing all recalculate from the combined quantity.
8. Flavor selectors were removed from the Products and Product Details UI; users select the SKU/product directly.
9. Existing Inventory, Movements, Games, Orders integration, dealer mapping and other project features are preserved from the supplied project.
